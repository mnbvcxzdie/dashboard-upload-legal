Privacy Policy

Dashboard Upload · Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Data we collect

When you connect TikTok, we receive from TikTok:

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), scope user.info.basic.
  • Access and refresh tokens to upload and publish on your behalf, scopes video.upload and video.publish.

We also store the videos, photos, captions and schedules you create in the app. The same applies to the other platforms you connect (Instagram, Threads, Facebook, YouTube).

2. How we use data

Data is used only to:

  • show which accounts are connected,
  • upload and publish the content you choose, at the time you schedule,
  • show upload status and errors.

We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Storage and security

Data is stored on the operator's own computer, not on third-party services. Tokens are never shown to anyone and are used only to call the official platform APIs. Media files are reachable only through random, unguessable links, so that platforms can fetch them while publishing.

4. Sharing

Your content is sent only to the platforms you select, such as TikTok. No other third party receives your data.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account is connected. You can:

  • disconnect the account in the app; its tokens are deleted immediately,
  • revoke access in TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services,
  • request deletion of all stored data via github.com/mnbvcxzdie/dashboard-upload-legal/issues; we delete it within 30 days.

6. Children

The service is not intended for users under 18.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
